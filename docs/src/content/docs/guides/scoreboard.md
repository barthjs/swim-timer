---

The scoreboard shows the live heat on a whole screen, for example on a monitor at the pool. The header
of the web interface and the Meet page open it in a new tab.

It shows the event, the heat, the number of laps, and the running time, and for every lane the competitor,
the distance covered, the time, and the rank.
